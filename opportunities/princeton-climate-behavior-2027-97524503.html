Overview

A five-day winter school bringing together early-career researchers, policymakers, and practitioners to turn behavioral science into practical interventions for climate action. Learn from leading researchers, work across disciplines and sectors, and develop an intervention addressing a real climate challenge.为期五天的冬季学校，汇聚早期职业研究人员、政策制定者和从业者，将行为科学转化为气候行动的实用干预措施。向顶尖研究者学习，开展跨学科、跨部门合作，并针对真实气候挑战制定一项干预方案。

Who should apply

We welcome applications from graduate students and professionals across the public- and private-sector who want to connect behavioral science with practical efforts to advance climate action.欢迎希望将行为科学与推进气候行动的实践相结合的研究生以及公共和私营部门的专业人士申请。
