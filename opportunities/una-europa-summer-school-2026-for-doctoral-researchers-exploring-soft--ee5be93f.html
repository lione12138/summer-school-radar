Overview

An in-person doctoral summer school at the University of Bologna on soft materials, spanning molecular systems through biomaterials.在博洛尼亚大学线下举办的博士暑期学校，主题涵盖从分子体系到生物材料的软物质研究。

Who should apply

Doctoral researchers enrolled or employed at an Una Europa partner university at the time of the summer school.面向暑期学校举办期间仍在 Una Europa 合作大学注册或任职的博士研究人员。
