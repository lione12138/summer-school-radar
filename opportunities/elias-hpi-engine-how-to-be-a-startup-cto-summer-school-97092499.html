Overview

An intensive four-day school on the CTO role in startups and technology companies, covering AI-first company building, technology strategy, leadership, lean analytics, and startup financing.为期四天的密集型暑期学校，聚焦初创公司与科技企业中的 CTO 职责，内容包括 AI 优先的公司建设、技术战略、领导力、精益分析和初创企业融资。

Who should apply

Master's students in computer science and PhD candidates; the 20 funded ELLIS/ELIAS places are reserved for affiliated PhD candidates and postdocs.面向计算机科学硕士生和博士生；其中 20 个 ELLIS/ELIAS 资助名额专门面向网络关联博士生及博士后。
