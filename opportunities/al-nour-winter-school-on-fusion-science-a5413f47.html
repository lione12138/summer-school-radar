Overview

The Al-Nour Winter School on Fusion Science runs from 4 to 8 January 2027 in Allan, Jordan. It introduces students and early-career scientists to fusion science. Accommodation and meals are covered for accepted applicants; travel support may be requested.阿尔努尔聚变科学冬季学校将于2027年1月4日至8日在约旦阿兰举行，面向学生和青年科研人员介绍聚变科学。录取者的住宿和餐食费用由项目承担，旅行资助可另行申请。

early-career scientists from a v​​ariety of backgrounds, like mathematics, physics, and engineering, to learn about this cutting-edge field from Arab and non-Arab experts i来自数学、物理和工程等不同背景的早期职业科学家，向阿拉伯和非阿拉伯专家学习这一前沿领域
