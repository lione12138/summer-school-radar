Overview

A five-day interdisciplinary school on trustworthy and responsible AI for drug discovery, covering robustness, privacy-aware machine learning, explainability, dual-use risks, cheminformatics, structural bioinformatics, and regulation.为期五天的跨学科暑期学校，聚焦用于药物发现的可信与负责任 AI，涵盖鲁棒性、隐私保护机器学习、可解释性、双重用途风险、化学信息学、结构生物信息学和监管。
