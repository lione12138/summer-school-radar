VIASM-IAMP Summer School 2027 on Probability and Mathematical PhysicsVIASM-IAMP 2027年概率与数学物理暑期学校

Overview

The goal of the summer school is to introduce selected topics from current research in probability and mathematical physics. In addition, the programme will include 10 contributed talks by young participants.暑校介绍概率与数学物理领域当前研究的若干专题，并安排 10 场由青年参与者贡献的报告。

Who should apply

General registration eligibility restrictions are not explicitly stated on the official page. The programme covers probability and mathematical physics and includes contributed talks by young participants.官网未明确列出一般注册者的资格限制。课程内容涵盖概率与数学物理，并安排青年参与者贡献的报告。

Courses and lecturers课程内容与讲者

Four mini-courses are announced: Frédéric Faure on dynamical Laplacians and Anosov manifolds; Thierry Lévy on determinantal methods; Satya Majumdar and Grégory Schehr on first-passage problems and stochastic resetting; Oanh Nguyen on random functions and their geometric and statistical behaviour.官网公布四门短课：Frédéric Faure 讲授 Anosov 流形上的动力学拉普拉斯算子；Thierry Lévy 讲授行列式相关专题；Satya Majumdar 与 Grégory Schehr 讨论首达时间、随机重置及其统计物理方法；Oanh Nguyen 介绍随机函数的几何与统计性质。
