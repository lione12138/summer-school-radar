Overview

This course aims to enhance understanding and application of the City-Wide Inclusive Sanitation (CWIS) approach in addressing urban sanitation challenges, promoting a paradigm shift towards sustainable sanitation service provision.本课程旨在加深对全市包容性环境卫生（CWIS）方法的理解与应用，以应对城市环境卫生挑战，推动向可持续环境卫生服务供给的模式转变。

Who should apply

Professionals working or aspiring to work in the urban sanitation sector, particularly those involved in projects promoting the Citywide Inclusive Sanitation (CWIS) approach. The course is also relevant for organizations在城市环境卫生领域工作或有意从事该领域工作的专业人士，尤其是参与推动全市包容性环境卫生（CWIS）方法项目的人员。本课程也适合相关组织
