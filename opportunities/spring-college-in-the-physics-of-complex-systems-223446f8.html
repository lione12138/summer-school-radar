Overview

The Spring College on the Physics of Complex Systems aims to expose students to a selection of topics at the forefront of research during an intensive, 4-week programme.复杂系统物理学春季学院旨在通过为期4周的密集课程，让学生接触研究前沿的精选课题。

Who should apply

The Spring College is part of the PCS Master programme and it is open to qualified Master and PhD students, and to young researchers.春季学院是PCS硕士项目的一部分，面向符合条件的硕士生、博士生和年轻研究人员开放。
