Overview

Research training on regional migration studies, examining human mobility, governance and socioeconomic transformations through regional and comparative perspectives.区域移民研究的研究培训，通过区域和比较视角考察人口流动、治理与社会经济转型。

Who should apply

The PhD School welcomes applications from doctoral researchers working on migration and human mobility from the disciplines of social sciences and all geographical areas.博士学校欢迎社会科学各学科、研究各地理区域移民与人口流动的博士研究人员申请。
