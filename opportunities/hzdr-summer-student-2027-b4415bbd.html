Overview

Every summer, for eight weeks between July and September, the HZDR welcomes international students to join our research teams and contribute to their projects.每年夏季7月至9月期间，HZDR欢迎国际学生进行为期八周的交流，加入我们的研究团队并为项目做出贡献。

Who should apply

The program is open to upper-level students from around the world who are enrolled to study natural sciences, life sciences, medicine, engineering, or related fields.该项目面向全球高年级学生，须已注册攻读自然科学、生命科学、医学、工程或相关领域。
