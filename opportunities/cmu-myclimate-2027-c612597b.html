Overview

MyClimate winter school: qualitative social science research on climate change, with lectures, field visits and participant presentations. The official page states '15 November 2027: Acceptance Announcements', after the school dates; this apparent date error needs confirmation from the organisers.MyClimate 冬季学校：关于气候变化的定性社会科学研究，包含讲座、实地考察和参与者展示。官方页面写有“2027年11月15日：录取通知”，该日期晚于学校举办时间；这一明显日期错误需向主办方确认。

Who should apply

Graduate students, emerging and independent scholars, researchers and activists from Myanmar and Thailand. Good working knowledge of English is required.来自缅甸和泰国的研究生、新兴及独立学者、研究人员和活动人士。要求具备良好的英语工作能力。
