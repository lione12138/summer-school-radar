Overview

The Winter School analyzes the phenomena of federalism, regionalism and multilevel governance from legal and political science perspectives. Each edition focuses on a specific topic, which is then approached in detail by legal scholars, political scientists and practitioners.冬季学校从法学和政治学视角分析联邦制、地区主义和多层次治理现象。每届聚焦一个特定主题，由法律学者、政治学者和实务工作者深入探讨。

Who should apply

The postgraduate program welcomes an international audience: young researchers and academics, post-docs and post-graduate students, civil servants from a local, regional or national government as well as employees of international organizations or NGOs.该研究生项目面向国际受众：青年研究人员和学者、博士后和研究生、地方、地区或国家政府公务员，以及国际组织或非政府组织雇员。

The 2027 academic focus2027 年课程重点

This edition examines federalism under a changing international order: constitutional resilience, security alliances, democratic backsliding and shifts in power between levels of government. Comparative cases include the EU and examples from several continents. Teaching combines lectures, workshops, questions and discussion; the working language is English.本届主题是变化中的世界秩序与联邦制，讨论宪政韧性、安全联盟、民主倒退，以及不同层级政府之间的权力变化。案例涉及欧盟和多个洲的多层级治理制度。教学结合讲座、专题工作坊、问答与讨论，全程使用英语。

Two cities and full attendance两地安排与出席要求

The first week takes place in Innsbruck and the second in Bolzano/Bozen. This is a single two-week programme: applicants cannot choose only one week, and online attendance is unavailable.第一周在奥地利 Innsbruck，第二周在意大利 Bolzano/Bozen。两周构成一个完整项目，不能只参加其中一周，也不提供线上参加方式；规划行程与住宿时需同时考虑两座城市。

Prepare the application before submitting提交前要准备什么

Submit the online form, not the preview PDF. It asks for education, experience, a motivation statement of at most 1,500 characters including spaces, and an academic essay of at most 3,500 characters. The essay must answer a listed question for one selected workshop. No additional documents are required.必须提交在线表单，填写预览 PDF 不算申请。表单包含教育与工作经历、最多 1,500 字符（含空格）的动机陈述，以及最多 3,500 字符的学术短文。短文需选择一个工作坊并回答对应问题；官网同时用 500 词说明篇幅，应兼顾字符限制。无需另附其他文件。建议先准备文稿，再一次性完成表单。

Actual costs and limited scholarships实际费用与有限奖学金

The EUR 300 participation fee includes materials, specified welcome meals and the inter-city transfer, but not accommodation, most meals or travel to the school. Optional 13-night room-and-breakfast packages cost an additional EUR 1,170 for a single room or EUR 962 for a twin room. A maximum of two full and three partial scholarships is advertised; even recipients pay EUR 150. Awards depend on merit and need.300 欧元参与费包含课程材料、官网列出的欢迎餐饮及两城间接驳，不含住宿、大部分餐饮和往返学校的差旅。主办方可安排 13 晚含早餐住宿，单人间另付 1,170 欧元，双人间另付 962 欧元。官网最多提供 2 个全额及 3 个部分奖学金名额，按学术表现与经济需要评选；获奖者仍需支付 150 欧元参与费，不能将奖学金视为人人可得。
