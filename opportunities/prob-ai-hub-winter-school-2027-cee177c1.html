Overview

A four-day Prob_AI Hub winter school covering causality, computational optimal transport, operator learning, reinforcement learning, machine learning for PDEs, and neural processes. Attendance is free; travel and accommodation are normally self-funded, with a limited Access Fund for eligible UK-based participants.Prob_AI Hub 举办的四天冬季学校，内容包括因果推断、计算最优传输、算子学习、强化学习、偏微分方程机器学习和神经过程。项目免费参加；交通和住宿通常自理，符合条件的英国参与者可申请有限的 Access Fund。

Who should apply

Open to applicants worldwide, with priority given to UK-based applicants.面向全球申请者，英国申请者优先。
