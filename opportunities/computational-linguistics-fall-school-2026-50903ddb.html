Overview

A biennial Computational Linguistics Fall School with two course weeks covering methodological and applied CL/NLP topics not typically offered in regular degree programmes.一项两年一届的计算语言学秋季学校，为期两个课程周，涵盖常规学位课程中较少开设的计算语言学与自然语言处理方法及应用主题。

Who should apply

Master's students and early-stage PhD students from the humanities and computer science.面向人文学科和计算机科学领域的硕士生及博士早期阶段学生。
