Spring College in the Physics of Complex Systems

ICTP and SISSA · ICTP Kastler Lecture Hall (AGH) Adriatico Guest House - Kastler Lecture Hall Riva Massimiliano e Carlotta, Grignano I - 34151 Trieste (Italy)

15 Feb – 12 Mar 2027 · 26 days 01 Nov 2026 in-person

Overview

The Spring College on the Physics of Complex Systems aims to expose students to a selection of topics at the forefront of research during an intensive, 4-week programme.

Who should apply

The Spring College is part of the PCS Master programme and it is open to qualified Master and PhD students, and to young researchers.
