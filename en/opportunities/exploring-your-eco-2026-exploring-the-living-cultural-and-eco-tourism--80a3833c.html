Exploring Your Eco 2026 – Exploring the living, cultural, and eco-tourism recreational environments of Konitsa, in northern Greece

Czech University of Life Sciences Prague, with Vikos-Aoos UNESCO Global Geopark and Trace Your Eco · Konitsa, Vikos-Aoos UNESCO Global Geopark, Greece

13 Jun – 21 Jun 2026 · 9 days 19 Apr 2026 in-person

Overview

A nine-day on-site summer school in Konitsa examining the living, cultural, and eco-tourism environments of the Vikos-Aoos UNESCO Geopark through research, field activities, and participant recommendations.
