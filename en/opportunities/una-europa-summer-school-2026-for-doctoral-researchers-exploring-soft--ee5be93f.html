Una Europa Summer School 2026 for doctoral researchers: Exploring Soft Materials – From Molecules to BioMaterials

Una Europa · University of Bologna Navile Campus, Bologna, Italy

29 Jun – 3 Jul 2026 · 5 days 13 Mar 2026 in-person

Overview

An in-person doctoral summer school at the University of Bologna on soft materials, spanning molecular systems through biomaterials.

Who should apply

Doctoral researchers enrolled or employed at an Una Europa partner university at the time of the summer school.
