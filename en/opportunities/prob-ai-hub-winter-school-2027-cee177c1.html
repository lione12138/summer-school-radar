Prob_AI Hub Winter School 2027

Prob_AI Hub · Fry Building, University of Bristol, Bristol, UK

5 Jan – 8 Jan 2027 · 4 days Deadline uncertain in-person

Overview

A four-day Prob_AI Hub winter school covering causality, computational optimal transport, operator learning, reinforcement learning, machine learning for PDEs, and neural processes. Attendance is free; travel and accommodation are normally self-funded, with a limited Access Fund for eligible UK-based participants.

Who should apply

Open to applicants worldwide, with priority given to UK-based applicants.
