IEEE RAS Summer School in Germany: Bridging AI and Robotics

IEEE Robotics and Automation Society (IEEE RAS) · Abtei Frauenwörth, Chiemsee, Germany

7 Sep – 11 Sep 2026 · 5 days 13 Jul 2026 in-person

Overview

A five-day in-person IEEE RAS summer school on cognitive robotics, bridging artificial intelligence, machine learning, perception, planning, and intelligent robotic systems.

Who should apply

early-career researchers, and professionals interested in the next generation of intelligent robotic systems

Why this status

5 days; priority region; trusted source layer 1.5; in-person

Official source

Original source evidence is retained below for verification.

Applications were open until 13 July 2026; registration until 31 July is only for accepted applicants. September 7–11, 2026 Official registration table: students pay USD 670 (RAS), USD 710 (IEEE), or USD 750 (non-IEEE); non-students pay USD 770 (RAS), USD 820 (IEEE), or USD 870 (non-IEEE), all including 19% VAT. Participants must pay the applicable registration fee; creating an IEEE account free of charge is unrelated to the school fee.
