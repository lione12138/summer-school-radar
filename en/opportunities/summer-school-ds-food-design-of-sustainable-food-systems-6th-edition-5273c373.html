Summer school DS-FOOD Design of sustainable food systems (6th edition)

Euroleague for Life Sciences (ELLS) · Alpine Study Centre located in Pieve Tesino, Via Rovigo, 7, 38050, TN, Italy

5 Jul – 12 Jul 2026 · 8 days 01 May 2026 in-person

Overview

An eight-day summer school on designing sustainable food systems at the Alpine Study Centre in Pieve Tesino, including lectures, field visits, accommodation, and partially covered dinners.

Who should apply

Students at the listed non-Italian ELLS promoting universities; other participants may attend for the stated registration fee.
