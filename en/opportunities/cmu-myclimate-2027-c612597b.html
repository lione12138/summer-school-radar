Call for Applications: MyClimate Winter School January 2027

Danish Institute for International Studies (DIIS) and Chiang Mai University · Chiang Mai, Thailand

14 Jan – 22 Jan 2027 · 9 days 30 Oct 2026 in-person

Overview

MyClimate winter school: qualitative social science research on climate change, with lectures, field visits and participant presentations. The official page states '15 November 2027: Acceptance Announcements', after the school dates; this apparent date error needs confirmation from the organisers.

Who should apply

Graduate students, emerging and independent scholars, researchers and activists from Myanmar and Thailand. Good working knowledge of English is required.

How this listing was assessed

low fee: approximately EUR 0; deadline appears open; 9 days; priority region
