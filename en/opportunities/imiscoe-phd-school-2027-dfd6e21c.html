IMISCOE PhD School 2027 — Regional Migration Studies: Dynamics of Human Mobility, Governance and Socioeconomic Transformations

GRITIM-UPF at Universitat Pompeu Fabra in collaboration with the EuroMedMig Marie Skłodowska-Curie PhD Project · Ciutadella Campus, Universitat Pompeu Fabra (UPF) in Barcelona, Spain

13 Sep – 17 Sep 2027 · 5 days 15 Nov 2026 in-person

Overview

Research training on regional migration studies, examining human mobility, governance and socioeconomic transformations through regional and comparative perspectives.

Who should apply

The PhD School welcomes applications from doctoral researchers working on migration and human mobility from the disciplines of social sciences and all geographical areas.
