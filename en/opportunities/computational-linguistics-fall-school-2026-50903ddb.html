Computational Linguistics Fall School 2026

University of Technology Nuremberg (UTN) · University of Technology Nuremberg, Nuremberg, Germany

21 Sep – 2 Oct 2026 · 12 days 06 Sep 2026 in-person

Overview

A biennial Computational Linguistics Fall School with two course weeks covering methodological and applied CL/NLP topics not typically offered in regular degree programmes.

Who should apply

Master's students and early-stage PhD students from the humanities and computer science.

Why this status

funding evidence: scholarship; deadline appears open; 12 days; priority region

Official source

Original source evidence is retained below for verification.

Registration Period Until September 6, 2026 September 21–October 2, 2026 After the 15 July early-bird deadline, the regular registration fee is EUR 150 for students and EUR 200 for non-students. Thanks to the support of the associations GSCL and DGfS, scholarships covering the registration fee will be available for selected participants.
