ELIAS & HPI Engine "How to Be a (Startup-) CTO" Summer School

HPI Engine & ELIAS Startup Opportunities · HPI Potsdam

31 Aug – 3 Sep 2026 · 4 days 28 Aug 2026 in-person

Overview

An intensive four-day school on the CTO role in startups and technology companies, covering AI-first company building, technology strategy, leadership, lean analytics, and startup financing.

Who should apply

Master's students in computer science and PhD candidates; the 20 funded ELLIS/ELIAS places are reserved for affiliated PhD candidates and postdocs.

Why this status

funding evidence: travel grant, accommodation; deadline appears open; 4 days; priority region
