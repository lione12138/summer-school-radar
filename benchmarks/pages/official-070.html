    CERN School of Computing

    • Computing.School@cern.ch

    Welcome to the 48th CERN School of Computing (CSC 2026)

    The school will take place from August 23 to September 5 in Liverpool, UK. This year's school is organized in collaboration with the Liverpool University, Department of Physics and will be hosted at the Liverpool University premises.

    Academic Programme

    The theme of the school is Scientific Computing: Theory and Practice. The two-week programme will consist of around 50 hours of lectures and hands-on exercises, covering three main themes:

    • Physics computing
    • Software engineering
    • Data technologies. 

    Students who pass the final optional exam will receive a diploma from the CSC, as well as ECTS credits.

    Other activities

    However, it's not all study; the social and sport programme is also a vital part of the School. We will have ample opportunities to explore and experience some of the great cultural, historical and natural attractions of Liverpool and its surroundings, including visits to nearby Wales! Have a look at the timetable, it will give you an idea of the school (please note that this timetable is still subject for change).

    The application for this school is now closed.

    Important dates

    • Wednesday 18 March - applications open
    • Wednesday 29 April (midnight UTC+2 / CEST) - deadline for applications
    • Wednesday 6 May - invitations sent to the selected participants
    • Wednesday 3 June - registration fee payment deadline
    • Sunday 23 August (afternoon/evening) - student arrivals in Liverpool, UK.
    • Saturday 5 September  (morning) - departure

    Who can apply?

    The School is aimed at postgraduate (ie. minimum of Bachelor's degree or equivalent) students, engineers, and scientists with a few years of experience in particle physics, computing, or in related fields. We welcome applications from all countries and nationalities. Applicants are responsible for ensuring that their home institute or employer covers their registration fee and travel cost, or, failing this, themselves. 

    We would like to thank our Local Organisers, the Department of Physics at Liverpool University.
