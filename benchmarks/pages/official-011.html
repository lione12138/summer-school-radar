VIASM-IAMP Summer School 2027 on Probability and Mathematical Physics

Time:

Venue/Location: Vietnam Institute for Advanced Study in Mathematics (VIASM), Hanoi

Objective

The Summer School in Probability and Mathematical Physics is part of a long-term initiative hosted by the Vietnam Institute for Advanced Study in Mathematics (VIASM) to support the development of probability and mathematical physics in Vietnam and neighboring countries. In particular, we aim to organize the school as a satellite event of the International Congress on Mathematical Physics (ICMP), which will take place in Da Nang in August 2027. The school itself will be held from July 23 to July 28, 2027.

The goal of the summer school is to introduce selected topics from current research in probability and mathematical physics. The programme will feature four mini-courses:

  1. A dynamical Laplacian on Anosov manifolds – Frédéric Faure (Mini-course 1)
  2. Determinantal matters – Thierry Lévy (Mini-course 2)
  3. From first-passage times to stochastic resetting: modern approaches in statistical physics – Satya Majumdar & Grégory Schehr (Mini-course 3)
  4. Geometric and statistical properties of random functions – Oanh Nguyen (Mini-course 4)

In addition, the programme will include 10 contributed talks by young participants.

Organizing Institutions 

  • International Research Laboratory France-Vietnam in Mathematics and its Applications, University of Tours;
  • Vietnam Institute for Advanced Study in Mathematics (VIASM).

  • Can Van Hao, Vietnam Academy of Science and Technology; 
  • Nguyen Viet Dang, Sorbonne Université, France; 
  • Nguyen Tien Dung, University of Science, Vietnam National University Ha Noi;
