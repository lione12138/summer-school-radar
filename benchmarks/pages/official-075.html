CODATA-RDA Research Data Science Summer School and Advanced workshops | (smr 4320)
Starts 9 Nov 2026
Ends 20 Nov 2026
Central European Time
ICTP
Denardo Lecture Hall (AGH)
Adriatico Guest House Riva Massimiliano e Carlotta, Grignano I - 34151 Trieste (Italy)
An ICTP Meeting

This activity focuses on building a range of data-related skills that are vital for addressing trans-disciplinary challenges in contemporary research work for participants from all disciplines and/or backgrounds from sciences to humanities. Topics to be covered include principles and practice of research data management including AI assisted RDM, authorship in the 21st century, open and responsible science, machine learning and artificial neural networks followed by selected advanced topics of Urban Data & AI assisted Data Analysis.

This is a practical hands-on activity with sessions on techniques and applications for both python and AI based coding and use of  local LLMs and AI tools for both Urban data and general AI assisted Data Analysis.


Topics
  • Unix environments and AI tools
  • AI assisted RDM
  • Python and AI Coding tools
  • Open and Responsible Research
  • Author Carpentry
  • Computational Infrastructure
  • Recommender systems
  • Artificial Neural Networks
  • Urban data 
  • AI assisted Data Analysis
