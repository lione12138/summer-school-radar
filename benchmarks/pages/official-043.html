Application

The intended audience of ALPS 2027 are graduate students (PhD students or advanced master students) in natural language processing or related fields. We also welcome other NLP practitioners working in industry or academia.

Application form: https://framaforms.org/alps-2027-application-form-1789055166-0

You will need a resumé (maximum length: 2 pages) and a cover letter. The cover letter should be written in English and explain your motivation in attending this advanced research school. We are unfortunately not capable of accepting all candidates, and this letter is a key component that helps us to ensure that the participant’s profiles are balanced and diverse.

In case you have problems accessing the form, please try accessing it through a VPN. If you have any issues with submitting your application, please contact us (see contact email here).

By applying, you commit to our code of conduct.

Important dates :

  • Application deadline: 11th October
  • Acceptance notification: 13th November
  • Winter School: 28th March to 2nd April 2027

The registration fees for the event encompass accommodation and full board at the conference venue, the Centre Paul Langevin.

Fees (to be confirmed):

– students: 700 euros
– academic non student: 900 euros
– industry & independents: 1300 euros
