INTERNATIONAL WINTER SCHOOL 2027

Additive Manufacturing From Theory to Practice

18 to 29 January 2027   Turin Italy

A two-week international programme for PhD candidates combining lectures, hands-on laboratories, a team project, technical visits and direct contact with academic and industrial experts.

Apply by 15 November 2026

AT A GLANCE

Build knowledge through practice

THE EXPERIENCE

From materials to industrial application

The Winter School develops a cross-disciplinary understanding of additive manufacturing across materials, processes, design, process control, post-processing and characterisation. Participants apply this knowledge in laboratories and in a team project that runs throughout the programme.

TURIN AND PIEDMONT

Study additive manufacturing in Turin

Teaching takes place across Politecnico di Torino campuses, IAM@PoliTO laboratories and industrial facilities. The programme also introduces participants to the architectural and cultural heritage of Turin and Piedmont.
