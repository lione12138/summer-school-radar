EU4MOFs training school – MOF tech transfer: market and industry perspective

  • Home
  • Events

  • EU4MOFs training school – MOF tech transfer: market and industry perspective

  • Apply by
    September 25, 2026 00:00 - September 25, 2026 23:55

EU4MOFs Action is organising a two-day training school exploring how Metal-Organic Frameworks (MOFs) can move from cutting-edge academic research towards industrial applications, commercialisation, and real-world market opportunities.

The event will bring together researchers, industry professionals, technology-transfer experts, and innovators to exchange knowledge and discuss key aspects of translating promising MOF technologies from the laboratory to the market.

Topics will include industry expectations and market needs, technology transfer and commercialisation pathways, scale-up and technology-readiness challenges, intellectual property and innovation, academia–industry collaboration, and career opportunities in industry and technology transfer.

When: 1-2 October 2026

Where: University of Birmingham, UK

Format: Hybrid

Registration form: click here to access it online

Registration deadline: 25 September 2026

Local organisers: Dr Selina Ambrose and Dr Lisa Sun, EU4MOFs WG6 Leaders

Science Communication Coordinator: Dr Olesia Kulyk, [email protected]

More information: eu4mofs.com -  eu4mofs.com/2026/09/01/birmingham
