Apply

The Princeton Winter School in Behavioral Science for Climate Action will bring together participants for five days of learning and collaborative intervention design.


We welcome applications from graduate students and professionals across the public- and private-sector who want to connect behavioral science with practical efforts to advance climate action.

Start your application

Application Deadline

November 15, 2026 | 11:59 PM EST

Winter School Dates

January 19-23, 2027

WHO SHOULD APPLY

Early-Career Researchers

Develop your research skills in behavioral science and climate action through expert-led training and hands-on challenges.

Policymakers, Advocates, and Change-makers

Translate behavioral insights into actionable policies, advocacy strategies, and media communications to drive effective climate action.

Industry Professionals

Learn how to apply behavioural insights to design and implement effective sustainability initiatives, drive organizational change, and engage employees in climate action.

We welcome early-career researchers and policy and practice professionals who want to use behavioral science to advance climate action.

Participation expectations

The program is designed as a cumulative, five-day experience. Participants should plan to attend the full week and contribute actively to lectures, discussions, group work, and the final presentations.

Costs and financial support
