The Australian Nuclear Science and Technology Organisation (ANSTO) and the Helmholtz Zentrum Berlin (HZB) together with Asia-Oceania Neutron Scattering Association (AONSA) are pleased to announce the ANSTO-HZB-AONSA Neutron School 2026. This will be held between 16-20 November 2026. This event is a unique combination of two established events, the ANSTO-HZB Training Course and AONSA Neutron School. 

 

The aim of this school is to provide the participants with both a broad overview of neutron scattering theory and knowledge of its application to a wide range of science areas. The course will consist of lectures and practicals, with the participants able to choose sessions using the neutron beam instruments of the Australian Centre for Neutron Scattering (ACNS) or learning the principles of chemical and bio deuteration or DFT calculations.

 

Subjects covered include: 

  • Basic theory
  • Instrumentation
  • Small-angle scattering
  • Reflectometry
  • Deuteration
  • Diffraction
  • Imaging
  • Residual stress and strain scanning
  • Inelastic scattering
  • Quasi-elastic scattering
  • Polarised neutrons and magnetism

 

There will also be a presentation covering ACNS Sample Environment capabilities and a tutorial on preparing instrument proposals. Participants will also have the opportunity to present their work to the lecturers, ACNS staff and, most importantly, each other at the school poster session and throughout the duration of the course. 

 

The ANSTO-HZB-AONSA Neutron School has strictly limited places and is particularly suited to those who are new to or have limited experience with neutron scattering but are willing to explore the opportunity of how to apply the techniques to their research. We invite applications from Honours, Masters and PhD students as well as early career researchers from both Australia and internationally, especially welcoming applications from Germany, and the Asia and Oceania region.

Successful Applicants

All successful applicants will be required to pay the registration fee of $750 inc GST (AUD) which is inclusive of:

  • All lectures and practical sessions
  • Daily return coach travel from the accommodation to ANSTO, Lucas Heights
  • Accommodation (check-in Sunday 15 Nov, check-out Fri 20 Nov) 
    • NOTE: this is a residential training course, hence it is expected that local participants will also stay at the hotel for the duration of the school. Please contact the Organisers if this presents a barrier to your participation.
  • Meals while onsite on Monday-Friday (morning tea, lunch, afternoon tea)
  • Poster session dinner on Tuesday, 17 November
  • Networking Dinner on Thursday, 19 November
  • Printed A0 (Portrait) poster (PDF must be sent no later than 1 November)

The registration fee does not include:

  • Cost of flights to/from Sydney, including any on the ground transport to the hotel on Sunday 15 Nov (some financial support might be available, subject to evaluation)
  • Any other meals, transport or accommodation not mentioned above

*Successful applicants will also be required to submit and pass the ANSTO security clearance requirements (this process can take up to 12 weeks) before the start of the course. All non-Australian citizens coming from abroad will require a valid entry visa. Please check requirements and timelines to ensure you are able to obtain your visa before travel.
